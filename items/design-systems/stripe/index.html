Stripe

About

A DESIGN.md reference for Stripe's public marketing surface — colour, type, spacing, radius, elevation and component tokens with the rules for using them. Unofficial and unaffiliated.

Designed for

  • Fintech and developer platforms with dense pricing and product detail
  • Giving an assistant exact colour, type, radius and elevation tokens to build with
  • Studying how a mature brand reduces its UI to a handful of rules
