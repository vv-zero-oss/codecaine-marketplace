About

A five-page template for a centralized social media manager run by an AI marketing team: floating workflow chips around a serif hero, a product window that unfolds on scroll, a pinned How-it-works tour, an AI Marketing Manager page with a streaming weekly brief, an AI Video Studio with storyboards and templates, a multi-channel scheduler with a best-time heatmap and approvals, and pricing with a comparison table.

Designed for

  • Social media management, scheduling and creator tools
  • AI marketing and AI video generation products
  • SaaS teams that want a calm, editorial, cream-and-ink landing page with night diagram sections
  • Seeing scroll-pinned tours, pixel wipes and streaming text the editor can drive
