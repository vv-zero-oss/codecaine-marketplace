About

A dark, metallic landing page for a virtual card company: machined-metal cards (chrome, titanium, champagne, graphite, copper) with brushing, film grain and a pointer-tracked highlight; two live 3D iPhones (React Three Fiber); a manifesto that lights up word by word; a pinned four-step “how it works”; a sideways-scrolling row of use cases; a customer video that zooms to fill the screen; a dotted world map, reviews, an FAQ and a champagne sign-up band.

Designed for

  • Fintech, card and wallet apps launching with a single clear offer
  • Mobile-first products that want their app shown on a real 3D phone rather than a flat screenshot
  • Scroll-told product pages: pinned steps, horizontal rows, a full-bleed video story
  • Seeing React Three Fiber, Motion and Lenis on one page the canvas editor can pause and edit
