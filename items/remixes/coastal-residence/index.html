About

Luna Residence — a cinematic one-page site for a small gated development on the coast: an arch that opens onto the hero, a day/night switch, pinned chapters, a sideways coastline story, residence types, amenities and an olive contact footer.

Designed for

  • Boutique residential developments and off-plan launches selling a handful of homes
  • Hotels, villas and retreats that want a slow, editorial scroll instead of a booking grid
  • Studios pitching a premium real-estate concept with pinned, scroll-driven chapters
  • Anyone after a Lenis + Framer Motion page with a strong condensed-serif voice
