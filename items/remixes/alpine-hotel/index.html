About

Hotel Arven — a one-page guide to a small, family-run hotel at the top of Zermatt, set like a printed hotel guide on warm paper: a cover with a fill-in availability line, the house's story, a pinned film that splits into the season calendar, a day's timetable with snowboard footage printed inside the type, a rate card, a drawn trail map of nearby spots, arrival routes and what's included, and a registration-card booking form.

Designed for

  • Small, independent hotels, inns and mountain lodges that take bookings direct
  • Hospitality brands that want an editorial, printed-matter feel instead of a template
  • Pages that need real information — seasons, rates, directions, what's included — laid out clearly
  • Anyone after a paper-textured Lenis + Framer Motion page with pinned video and text-clipped footage
