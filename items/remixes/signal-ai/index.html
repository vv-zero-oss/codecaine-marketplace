About

A sky-blue and white landing page for a frontier AI lab: a serif headline that types its own verb, a live product bento, pixel-mosaic edges that fade into sky washes, one API in three languages, and the sections a launch page needs: steps, reasons to switch, a platform split, testimonials, FAQ and a closing call.

Designed for

  • AI model, API or developer-platform launch pages
  • Products with several modalities (chat, code, voice, image, agents) to show at once
  • Teams who want a calm, white, editorial look instead of a dark gradient hero
  • Studying editor-ready motion: typed headline, looping previews, count-ups
