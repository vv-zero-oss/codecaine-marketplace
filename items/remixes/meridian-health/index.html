About

A soft pastel-gradient landing page for a connected health-coach app, with working phone and watch mockups: scrub a strain chart, tap a sleep stage, connect lab providers, switch the coach's voice and chat, log meals, flip through biological age, cycle, lifts and a journal — plus three scroll-driven chapters (a manifesto that reads itself in, a pinned day-with-Meridian timeline, a horizontal signal ribbon), privacy, reviews and a closing call.

Designed for

  • Health, fitness, wellness and wearable app launch pages
  • Mobile-app landing pages that need the product itself on show, not screenshots
  • Teams who want device mockups whose screens are live, interactive UI
  • Studying editor-ready motion: floating chips, marquees, count-ups, rings, an auto-advancing feature accordion
