About

An editorial portfolio for a working photographer: home, portfolio with category filter, a page per gallery, about, services with FAQ, and an enquiry form.

Designed for

  • Wedding, portrait and editorial photographers showing whole stories rather than single frames
  • Studios that sell packages and want pricing and FAQs on one page
  • Anyone who needs an enquiry form without a back end on day one
  • Starting a multi-page site the editor can read component by component
