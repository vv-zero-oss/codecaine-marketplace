About

A loud, photograph-led storefront for Truant, a Lake Superior single-origin roaster: Take the long way stacked huge in a round-shouldered condensed poster face over full-bleed photography, a fixed right-hand rail, warm paper, one tomato accent and soft low shadows. Four coffee series stack over one another as you scroll, the mission lights up word by word before a photograph opens out to fill the window, NEVER RUN DRY tumbles together letter by letter, and every bag is drawn and typeset live in SVG. A live roast curve you can scrub, a working brew timer, a real cart with a discount code and checkout, and a subscription you can skip, pause and cancel — plus Shop (filters, quick view, a taste map), Subscribe (a builder with a pinned live order), Field notes (a pinned origin map and a price ledger) and a live brand guide.

Designed for

  • Coffee roasters, food and drink brands, and DTC subscription shops
  • Storefronts that want oversized poster typography over full-bleed photography
  • Story-led home pages with sticky stacking sections and scroll-scrubbed reveals
  • Seeing working mockups — drawn product packaging, a cart, a subscription builder — built as editor-controllable components
