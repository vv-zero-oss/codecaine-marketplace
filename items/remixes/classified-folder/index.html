About

One cobalt folder on a warm desk that keeps one secret: hover and the cover swings open on its hinge while a sheet marked "Do not open" slides out an inch; click and the sheet is pulled out, turned over in the air and laid on the cover so its other side can be read. A single Motion component with every knob as a prop, and a style guide.

Designed for

  • A playful empty state, 404 or easter egg that rewards curiosity
  • Revealing terms, a secret code or launch notes behind one click
  • Showing how a 3D card flip and a hinged cover are built with Motion
  • A starting point for any 'open the folder' interaction
