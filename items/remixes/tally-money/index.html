About

A personal-finance landing page: inked-object hero with payment toasts, a three-bank link scene, a dense dashboard and phone, a live payment feed, self-typing search, proof, pricing with a border beam, FAQ and a closing — authored as named components for the editor.

Designed for

  • Launching a money, budgeting or banking app
  • A calm, friendly fintech look with real product mockups
  • Motion built as editable components: Framer Motion and Lenis
  • Starting a marketing site with a /brand style guide
