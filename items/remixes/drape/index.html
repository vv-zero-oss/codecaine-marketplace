About

A landing page for an AI fitting room: a three.js hero where every outfit is a pencil sketch drawn by a shader until a selection box opens over it and shows the real garment, a scroll-scrubbed pull-back into a sunlit room, a pinned four-step toolkit that recolours a garment into sixteen colourways in the page, live colour, fit and size controls, stories, parallax features, FAQ and a clay footer.

Designed for

  • Fashion, retail and AI try-on products that want to show the product working rather than describe it
  • Seeing a three.js shader carousel, scroll-scrubbed zooms and pinned steps as editor-ready components
  • Pages that mix a light drafting-paper hero with dark product sections and one warm accent
