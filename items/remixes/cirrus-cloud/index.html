About

A pixel-matrix site for Cirrus, cloud development environments: white paper under a 9px grid and a palette drawn only as squares — a night server-room hero with CDN packets travelling from origin to edge racks, isometric voxel server cards, a DNA-to-organism-to-tree life cycle for clone/boot/build/ship, a menubar with a pixel-icon product menu, product, network, pricing, changelog and brand-guideline pages, and footers with a different city skyline in line art over a playable Tetris skyline.

Designed for

  • Developer tools, cloud platforms and infrastructure products
  • Editorial, argument-led product pages with long reading sections
  • Pages that want generative pixel art instead of stock illustration
  • Seeing canvas animations built as editor-controllable components the Motion switch can stop
