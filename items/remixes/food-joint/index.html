About

A loud one-page site for a restaurant: a preloader that flicks through the menu, a hero photo in a morphing SVG cushion under huge type, a scroll-inked statement with food set into the words, a tabbed menu with a pointer-chasing blob preview, a zoom through a word cut out over a wood-fire film, a pinned sideways walk through the kitchen, stickers you can move, and a booking form.

Designed for

  • Restaurants, burger and fried-chicken joints, bakeries and street-food kitchens with a big personality
  • Menus that people want to browse, with prices, heat levels and a picture for every dish
  • Places that sell the kitchen as much as the plate — fire, wood, fryers and films of them
  • Taking table bookings without a back end on day one
