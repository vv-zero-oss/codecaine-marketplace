About

A calm, white-and-warm-grey fintech site for Kestrel, a spend platform run by agents: a golden-ratio type scale with tight negative tracking, one lime button, soft layered shadows and dot-grid product panels. An autoplaying hero stage (card dispensed, receipts matched, an invoice dropped and auto-coded), a customer wall that opens into photo stories, a working product bento, a pinned scroll where six disconnected tools collapse into one ledger, a pinned dollar-flow walkthrough, a live policy agent, an approval-workflow builder and a dotted globe, a savings calculator and drifting testimonials — plus Platform, Pricing and Customers pages and a live brand guide.

Designed for

  • Fintech, spend management, payments and finance-ops products
  • B2B SaaS pages that need to show the product working, not screenshots of it
  • Story-led landing pages with sticky, scroll-scrubbed sections
  • Seeing autoplaying scenes, a scroll-pinned stage and interactive mockups built as editor-controllable components
