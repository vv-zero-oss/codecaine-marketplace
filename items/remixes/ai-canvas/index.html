About

A launch page for an AI canvas tool: a night-black WebGL infinite canvas that never stops drifting — photos, labelled frames, live pages and an agent at work, with velocity motion blur — washing out to a white page of blur-in scenes, a stepped-word mauve panel, a pinned three-step product walk, gathering multiplayer cursors, an agent wiring a button to its tokens, and one enormous black call to action.

Designed for

  • AI design, canvas and prototyping tools launching or raising
  • Products whose pitch is an endless workspace: whiteboards, moodboards, site builders, agent studios
  • Pages that tell the story one short scene at a time rather than in feature grids
  • Seeing a WebGL canvas the editor's Motion switch can stop, with every knob as a prop
