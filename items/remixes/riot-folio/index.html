About

A loud portfolio for an independent builder — electric ultramarine, six saturated project tones, duotone photography, hero cards that fly into the work grid on scroll, odometer stats and a filterable project archive.

Designed for

  • Designers, engineers and consultants who want a portfolio people remember
  • Showing a handful of case studies properly and the rest as a searchable archive
  • A bold, colourful starting point that is still quiet enough to read
  • Trying scroll-linked motion that the editor can name, tune and pause
