About

A dark, iris-lit landing page for an AI support platform whose agents improve their own metrics — scanline display type, a voice-reactive prompt box that types, thinks and answers, auto-advancing product walkthroughs, a dotted language map and a customer story, authored as named components the editor can read.

Designed for

  • Launching an AI or agent product that sells on a measurable outcome
  • A dark, product-led marketing site with live-looking dashboard mockups
  • Designers who want to restyle atmosphere, type and motion from tokens
  • Trying the editor's motion and action channels on a motion-heavy page
